Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · Updated November 23, 2026 (the optional AI features and Sign in with Apple; MUSQ Pro; Apple Health, health answers, Balance and voice logging; Lifetime no longer sold)

This policy explains what information the MUSQ iOS app ("MUSQ", "the app") uses and why. MUSQ is made by Cuong Sam Manh ("we", "us"). Where this policy says iPhone, the same goes for MUSQ on an iPad. Contact: Manhcuong5311@gmail.com.

In short: what you enter in MUSQ (your workouts, your body details, your health answers and your daily logs) and any Apple Health data you let MUSQ read stay on your iPhone. The app doesn't need an account. Only if you use one of its optional AI features (section 3), sign in with Apple and agree first, does that feature send what it needs through MUSQ's server to OpenAI. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device and usage data to serve them. You can limit that, or turn the ads off with MUSQ Premium or Pro.

1. Information that stays on your device

When you set up MUSQ and use it, the app saves the following only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • Your onboarding answers: goal, sex (optional), year of birth, height, weight, daily activity, goal weight and pace, training days per week, the weekdays you train, preferred split, and any body measurements you choose to add (such as body fat or waist).
  • The health and safety answers you choose to give, such as sore joints, health conditions, a pregnancy, a recent birth or breastfeeding, and your answers to the short safety questions (for example about chest pain or fainting). MUSQ uses them on your device to adjust your calorie plan, to swap or flag exercises that may not suit you, and to suggest when to check with a doctor first. Only your sore joints and health conditions (a pregnancy or a recent birth included) can go to the AI coach, and only if you turn that on (section 3).
  • The name MUSQ greets you by, if you add one.
  • Your workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, dates, and the recovery estimates calculated from them.
  • The water and weigh-ins you log on the Today screen.
  • What you add yourself for Balance and the quick logs: meals you enter by hand or log from a meal scan (section 3), walks you add when you had no phone or Watch with you, your evening eating check-ins, your “slept about…” estimates, and any week you pause. Hand-added walks are kept in MUSQ with your other logs and are never written to Apple Health.
  • Your settings (units, training experience, rest timer, water target, reminders and their times, your Balance choices, and your Apple Health goals and calorie-plan choice, section 2) and the exercises you save.

We don't receive, upload, sell or share this information, except what you choose to send with an AI feature (section 3); your health answers go only if you turn them on there. None of it is ever passed to advertisers. Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup on your computer) according to your iOS settings; we can't read those backups. Deleting the app deletes it from your iPhone. Your chat with the AI coach and your goal photo are also kept only on your iPhone, but out of your backups (section 3).

If you allow notifications, MUSQ schedules local notifications on your device: the rest timer's alert, and the water, weigh-in, evening check-in and wind-down reminders and the Sunday check-in on your goal, if you turn them on. Nothing is sent to a server.

Balance

Balance, MUSQ's picture of your training, eating and sleep over the last 7 days, is worked out on your iPhone each time from the logs above and, if you connect it, your Apple Health sleep (section 2). The score itself isn't saved, and it is never sent to advertisers. If you ask MUSQ's coach how to improve it, your numbers go with that question as described in section 3, and your sleep only if you choose to include it.

Logging by voice

You can log water, a walk or a meal by speaking, or say the note for a meal scan. On iOS 26 and later, MUSQ uses the microphone only while its mic button is on, and Apple's speech recognition turns your words into text on your iPhone. MUSQ never saves the audio or sends it anywhere, and only the text you confirm is used; a spoken meal-scan note goes with the scan as text (section 3). On earlier versions MUSQ doesn't use the microphone itself: you can dictate with the keyboard's microphone, which is Apple's feature and follows your dictation settings and Apple's Privacy Policy.

2. Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional, and MUSQ works fully without it. If you connect it, MUSQ asks Apple's permission and reads only the types you allow on Apple's Health screen:

  • Steps and walking + running distance.
  • Active energy: the calories you burn by moving, usually measured by an Apple Watch.
  • Sleep: only the time you were asleep, and the sleep stages an Apple Watch records.
  • Workouts, so that the energy your Apple Watch (or another app) measured during a MUSQ session can be shown with it.

MUSQ only reads: it never asks to write to Apple Health, and never changes or deletes anything in it.

MUSQ uses this data on your device, to:

  • show your steps, distance, active energy and last night's sleep on the Today screen, and your step and sleep trends in Progress;
  • set how active your calorie plan assumes you are, from your average daily steps over the last two weeks, in place of the answer you gave when you set up the app;
  • work out the Sleep part of your Balance from your recent nights (section 1);
  • estimate the calories of your walking from your steps when Apple Health has no active energy, shown as an estimate and never added to your calorie target.

Your Apple Health data stays on your device. MUSQ never shares it with Google AdMob, any other advertiser or any data broker, and it is never used for advertising or marketing. The one exception is yours to make: if you include your sleep in a Balance question to MUSQ's coach, those sleep numbers go through MUSQ's server to OpenAI with that question and your next ones in that chat, and stay in the chat on your iPhone until you clear it (section 3). No other Apple Health data is ever sent.

MUSQ doesn't save your Health readings: it reads them again from Apple Health whenever you open the app, so MUSQ never puts them in iCloud or in your device backups. The only exception is sleep numbers you send in a question to the coach, which stay in that chat on your iPhone (kept out of backups) until you clear it. Otherwise the app keeps only your step and sleep goals, your reminder times (including a wind-down time you accept from MUSQ's suggestion, which is based on when you usually wake up), and the activity level your steps last set for the calorie plan, with your other settings (section 1). Apple Health itself stores your data under your Health and iCloud settings, as described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Turning it off

You can change what MUSQ may read at any time: in the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps under Privacy, then MUSQ; or go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › MUSQ. Turning access off stops MUSQ from reading new data; nothing in Apple Health is deleted, and the calorie plan goes back to the answer you gave at setup.

3. AI features (optional)

MUSQ has three optional features that use AI. They, and the MUSQ account they need, are the only parts of the app that send anything to MUSQ's server or to OpenAI, and only when you use them or are signed in (ads in the free version are covered in section 4):

  • Ask MUSQ, the AI training coach (MUSQ Premium and Pro);
  • meal scans, which estimate a meal's foods, grams and calories from a photo (MUSQ Pro);
  • the goal photo, an AI edit of a photo of you that shows you at the fitness goal you pick (adults only; your first one is free, and Premium and Pro make more each year).

In short: an AI feature sends nothing until you've signed in with Apple and agreed to its own consent screen, which names OpenAI and lists what the feature sends. MUSQ's server passes each request to OpenAI and sends the answer back to your iPhone; it doesn't store your photos, questions, answers or meals. OpenAI may keep what it receives for up to 30 days to check for abuse, and doesn't use it to train its models. Your chat, your goal photo and the meals you log stay on your iPhone. None of it is used for ads.

Your MUSQ account

The AI features need a MUSQ account, made with Sign in with Apple (in the app, under Profile › Account, or when a feature asks). The rest of MUSQ works without one. When you sign in:

  • Apple gives MUSQ a user ID, which Apple makes for MUSQ's developer (other developers get a different one) and which stays the same for your Apple Account, and an email address, because MUSQ asks for one. On Apple's screen you choose whether that is your real address or a private relay address (Hide My Email). MUSQ doesn't ask Apple for your name.
  • Google's Firebase Authentication makes your MUSQ account from these. It keeps a MUSQ account ID, your Apple user ID, that email address, and when the account was made and last signed in. MUSQ's server doesn't copy the email into its own records, we don't send email to it, and the app shows the address only to you, in Profile.
  • So that MUSQ's server knows your plan, the app sends it the App Store's signed records of your current MUSQ purchases: when you sign in, when MUSQ starts while you're signed in, after a purchase, and whenever the server's record needs catching up. Apple's records list the product, its dates and transaction IDs, and details such as the store country and price. The server checks Apple's signature and keeps only a plan record: the product, the plan (Premium or Pro), its App Store transaction IDs, whether it's active and when it ends. MUSQ never sees your payment details.

What each feature sends, and when

Each feature asks for your OK before its first use, on a screen that names OpenAI and lists what it sends. Until you agree, it sends nothing. After that, a request is sent only when you tap to send it: Send, a suggested question or Try again in the chat, Read this meal, or Show my goal. With each coach question, meal scan and goal-photo edit, OpenAI also gets a coded ID, made from your MUSQ account ID with a one-way hash, so it can spot abuse. It doesn't contain your name or email, and OpenAI can't tell from it who you are.

  • Ask MUSQ. Your question and the rest of the conversation (its last 20 messages, yours and the coach's, so the coach can follow it), together with:
    • your goal, age, sex, height, weight, training experience, split and training days a week;
    • your plan's calories and macros, worked out from your answers;
    • the workouts you logged in MUSQ in the last two weeks: dates, muscle groups, exercises, sets, and the reps and weight of your heaviest set (or your longest hold);
    • only if you turn on Also send my health answers: your sore joints (knee, lower back, shoulder, wrist or elbow) and health conditions (high blood pressure, a heart condition, diabetes, kidney disease, osteoporosis, a pregnancy or a birth in the last year). It starts off, and you can turn it on or off from the chat's menu, under What MUSQ shares. Nothing else from your health answers is sent: not your answers to the safety questions, and not details such as how far along a pregnancy is or whether you're breastfeeding.
  • Ask MUSQ from Balance. If you tap Ask MUSQ in Balance, MUSQ writes a question into the chat box with your last 7 days: your Balance score, your goal, your training numbers, the number of days you hit your protein, calorie and water targets, and your weight trend or key-lift progress. You see all of it, and nothing is sent until you tap Send. Your sleep numbers (from Apple Health or your own estimates) are in it only if you turn on Include my sleep, which is off each time. Once sent, the question is part of the conversation, so it also goes with your next questions in that chat while it is among the last 20 messages; clearing the chat removes it.
  • Meal scans. The photo you take or pick, and the note you add if any (typed, spoken or tapped in, up to 200 characters). Not your name, workouts, body details or health answers.
  • Goal photo. The photo you take or pick and the look you choose: sex, muscle and body fat. Before it's sent you confirm that the photo shows only you and that you are 18 or older. MUSQ's server also receives your birth year, checks that you're an adult, and doesn't keep it or pass it on.

Requests also include your time zone, so your daily or yearly allowance resets at your midnight, and, for the coach and meal scans, the app's language, so the answer comes back in English or Vietnamese. MUSQ's server uses them for that request only and doesn't store them. Like any internet request, each one also reaches Google with your IP address and a short description of your device (app version, iOS version and device model).

Your photos

MUSQ uses the camera only when you tap Take photo, and Apple's photo picker gives it only the photo you pick, never the rest of your library. Photos are resized on your iPhone and sent without their location or camera details. Before a goal photo is sent, the app checks on your iPhone that no second person is close to the camera in it. MUSQ's server holds a photo only while it handles your request, and never saves it. It checks each goal photo with OpenAI's moderation tool first, and OpenAI won't edit photos its safety rules don't allow.

An AI edit redraws the whole picture, face included, so MUSQ puts your own face back on your iPhone: Apple's Vision framework finds the face in your photo and in the edit, and the app lines them up and blends your original face in. The face positions it finds are used only for this, in the app's memory, and are never saved or sent. If it can't find your face clearly, the app says so and keeps the edit as it came. The finished goal photo, the photo it was made from and your goal are kept only on your iPhone, out of your backups, until you replace or delete them. The goal photo goes to your photo library only if you save it there, which asks permission only to add photos. It is an illustration of a possible look, not a prediction.

Who handles it, and where

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) runs MUSQ's server for us in Google's Singapore region, where the server's few records (below) are stored. Firebase Authentication, which holds your MUSQ account, runs in Google's US data centers. Service logs are kept in Google Cloud Logging.
  • OpenAI writes the coach's answers, reads meal photos and makes the goal-photo edit. MUSQ's server sends it only what is listed above. OpenAI is a US company and may process this data in the United States or other countries where it operates.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and signs App Store purchases, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Google and OpenAI work for us as service providers. They handle this data to run these features for MUSQ, to keep their services safe and to meet the law, not for their own advertising. OpenAI doesn't use the data MUSQ sends to train its models.

Your health information and the coach

Your health answers go to the coach only while Also send my health answers is on, and sleep from Apple Health only in a Balance question you include it in. Both start off. Steps, distance, active energy and Watch workouts are never sent. The calories and macros sent with every question come from your answers alone. A Balance question does say on how many days your eating matched your calorie target, and that target, as on Today, can follow your Apple Health steps; the steps themselves aren't sent. MUSQ's coach gives general training guidance, not medical advice. Health data sent this way is used only to answer you: never for advertising or marketing, and never sold.

Changing your mind, and deleting your account

  • Stop sharing. Profile › Account › AI data sharing › Withdraw turns off your OK for every AI feature, and each one asks again before it sends anything. In the chat, What MUSQ shares › Stop sharing does the same for the coach and turns your health answers off. What OpenAI already has is deleted on OpenAI's schedule (see the table above).
  • Delete your account. Go to Profile › Account › Delete account. After you confirm, Apple asks once more; MUSQ then asks Apple to revoke its Sign in with Apple access, deletes your plan record and your MUSQ account, withdraws your AI consents and signs you out. You can also email us and we'll delete it.
  • What's left after you delete it: what OpenAI still holds, until OpenAI deletes it (see the table above); the usage counts on your purchase until they expire; service logs for up to 30 days; Google's backups for a limited time; any record of a refunded purchase; and, if you made your free goal photo (or tried to), its count. That count is stored under a one-way hash of your Apple user ID, with no account ID, name or email, and can't be turned back into your Apple user ID. If you sign in again with the same Apple Account, MUSQ recognizes the hash and knows the free photo is used, which is why it's kept. On your iPhone, your workouts, chat and goal photo stay until you delete them or the app. Your subscription is separate: it keeps renewing until you cancel it in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
  • Deleting the app doesn't delete your MUSQ account. iOS also keeps your sign-in on your iPhone (in its keychain) after the app is deleted, so if you install MUSQ again, you're still signed in. To delete the account, use Profile before you delete the app, or email us; to remove only the sign-in, sign out in Profile first.

Reporting what the AI made

Report this image on a goal photo, and Report this answer when you press and hold one of the coach's answers, open an email to us in your mail app (for an answer, with its text). Nothing is sent until you send it, and we use it only to look into your report.

Adults only, and children

The goal photo is for adults. MUSQ offers it only if the birth year in your profile means you turn at least 19 this year, which means you are at least 18, and MUSQ's server checks the birth year again. You must also confirm that the photo shows only you and that you're 18 or older. It isn't offered during a pregnancy, or after a birth until your doctor or midwife has cleared you to exercise (and not in the first six weeks). Like the rest of MUSQ, the coach and meal scans are not directed at children (section 7).

Never for ads or tracking

What the AI features send is used only to give you the answer, estimate or photo you asked for, to count your allowance and to keep the service secure. It is never shared with Google AdMob or any other advertiser, never used for advertising, marketing or tracking, and never sold.

Security

The app reaches MUSQ's server only over encrypted connections. Only MUSQ's server code can read or write its database; the app can't. The key MUSQ uses with OpenAI is kept on the server, never in the app. Requests can also carry a token from Google's Firebase App Check, which uses Apple's App Attest to confirm they come from the real MUSQ app; the token holds no personal information.

Legal bases (GDPR and UK GDPR)

Where these laws apply, sending your data to OpenAI for a feature relies on your consent, given on that feature's screen, and for health information on your explicit consent: on the coach's screen, and through the switches for your health answers and your sleep. You can withdraw it at any time, as described above. Your account, the purchase check and the usage counts are needed to provide the features you ask for (our contract with you). Service logs, refund records and the free goal photo's count rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and preventing abuse. Google and OpenAI may process data outside your country, including in Singapore, the United States and other countries where they operate.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of MUSQ is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, which may include:

  • Device identifiers: the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and the identifier for vendors (IDFV).
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown and whether they were tapped.
  • Device and diagnostic information: device model, OS version, app version, language, and performance and crash data related to ads.

The SDK never receives your workouts, your body details, your health answers, your Apple Health data or anything the AI features send.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ads may come from Google or from third-party ad networks that Google works with.

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency. MUSQ asks with Apple's tracking prompt before it may use your IDFA for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but are not personalized using the IDFA. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. There, MUSQ shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested, and you can change your choices at any time in the app under Profile › Privacy choices.
  • MUSQ Premium or Pro. With either plan, the app stops loading ads and the Google Mobile Ads SDK is not started.

5. Purchases

MUSQ Premium (a yearly subscription) and MUSQ Pro (monthly or yearly, with more of the AI features) are sold through Apple's App Store and renew automatically until you cancel. The one-time Lifetime purchase is no longer sold; if you bought it earlier, you keep Premium. Apple processes the payment, and we never see your payment details. On your iPhone, the app checks with Apple which plan this Apple Account holds. If you sign in for the AI features, the app also sends MUSQ's server Apple's signed records of your MUSQ purchases, so the server can check your plan and its allowances (section 3). Apple's handling of your purchase is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. What we don't do

  • The app doesn't require an account: only the optional AI features need one, made with Sign in with Apple (section 3). MUSQ never asks Apple for your name and never asks for your contacts; a name you add for MUSQ to greet you by stays on your iPhone (section 1). The only email address the app gets is the one Sign in with Apple gives it, if you sign in. Apart from that and emails you send us, joining the website waitlist (section 10) is the only time we receive an email address.
  • MUSQ reads Apple Health only with your permission, never writes to it, doesn't save its readings, and never uses Health data for advertising or marketing. The only Health data that ever leaves your iPhone is sleep numbers you choose to send with a Balance question to the coach (sections 2 and 3).
  • MUSQ uses your camera only when you take a photo for a meal scan or a goal photo, gets only the photos you pick, and adds a goal photo to your photo library only when you ask. It doesn't access your precise location. MUSQ uses the microphone only while its mic button is on, recognizes your words on your device, and never saves or sends the audio (section 1).
  • We don't sell your personal information. Your workouts, body details, health answers and Apple Health data are shared with no one except OpenAI, which gets only what you send with an AI feature, through MUSQ's server (section 3).

7. Children

MUSQ is a general-audience fitness app and is not directed at children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us. The goal photo is for adults only, and the other AI features, like the rest of MUSQ, are not directed at children (section 3).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. You can change your answers in Profile at any time, and deleting the app deletes what MUSQ saved on your iPhone (section 1), apart from a sign-in to your MUSQ account (section 3). You can delete your MUSQ account in the app (Profile › Account › Delete account) or by emailing us; section 3 explains what remains. MUSQ keeps no copy of your Apple Health data, apart from sleep numbers you sent the coach, which stay in the chat on your iPhone until you clear it; you view and delete your Health data in the Health app. Advertising data is collected and held by Google. Use the choices above or Google's My Ad Center. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis for ads is your consent, collected through Google's consent message, and our legitimate interest in funding the free version of the app. For the AI features, see the legal bases in section 3. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Security and retention

Data on your device is protected by iOS, including device encryption when a passcode is set. Apart from the few records the AI features need (section 3) and emails you send us, we keep no copies of your data: what the AI features send passes through MUSQ's server without being stored. Google's retention of advertising data is described in its privacy policy.

10. The website and its waitlist

The MUSQ 3D exercise guide at musq.app lets you join a waitlist for the app. If you do:

  • We store the email address you enter, the time you signed up and the page you signed up from, in a Google Sheet that only we can open. Google hosts it under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • We use your email only to tell you when MUSQ launches and about important updates to it. We never sell or share it.
  • You can unsubscribe from any email, or ask us to delete your address at any time by writing to us from that address. The legal basis where the GDPR applies is your consent, which you give by submitting the form.
  • The site shows only the total number of people on the waitlist, never who they are.

The guide pages load the 3D viewer from jsDelivr and send signups to Google Apps Script; like any website request, these services receive your IP address and browser details. The site counts page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies, does not track you across sites and does not identify individual visitors; Cloudflare receives the page address, your browser type and your IP address to count the visit. The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It remembers in your browser's own storage that you joined, so the form doesn't ask again.
