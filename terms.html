  • AI can be wrong. The coach's answers and meal estimates are made by AI and can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. A meal estimate from a photo can be off by 20% or more, so check the foods and portions before you log them. The coach gives general training guidance, not medical advice: it doesn't diagnose or treat anything, and it doesn't replace a doctor, physiotherapist or dietitian. For pain, an injury, an illness, a pregnancy or medication, ask a doctor, and stop any exercise that hurts.

It shows a possible look for the goal you pick, not what you will look like or when: real progress depends on your training, food, sleep and genetics. Never use a photo of a child, a photo of someone else or a photo you don't have the right to use, and don't use photos or notes for anything sexual, violent or hateful.
