Common questions

Is MUSQ free?

Yes, MUSQ is free to download and use, with ads. Every exercise with its 3D model and every key tip, workout logging, your history and recovery estimates are free, along with one saved preset per muscle group.

What does MUSQ Premium add?

The common mistakes drawn over the lifter and Form Comparison, Preset 2 and 3 for every muscle group, and no ads. It comes as a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock.

I bought Premium (or Remove Ads) but it isn't active

Open Profile › MUSQ Premium and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. It can take a moment for a new purchase to reach the app. An earlier Remove Ads purchase counts as Premium.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, choose MUSQ and tap Cancel Subscription. Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. You can also open this screen from Profile › MUSQ Premium › Manage Subscription in the app.

What happens when Premium ends?

The Premium features lock again and ads return. Nothing is deleted: your workouts, history and saved presets stay on your iPhone.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my workout history stored?

Only on your iPhone. MUSQ has no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes your history.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it, use Profile › Privacy choices. To stop apps from using your device's advertising identifier, turn off Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking › Allow Apps to Request to Track.
