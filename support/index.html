Common questions

Is MUSQ free?

Yes, MUSQ is free to download and use, with ads. Every exercise with its 3D model and every key tip, workout logging, your history and recovery estimates are free, along with one saved preset per muscle group, the Today screen with your calorie plan, and your first goal photo (for adults).

What do MUSQ Premium and MUSQ Pro add?

Premium adds the common mistakes drawn over the lifter, Preset 2 and 3 for every muscle group and no ads, plus Ask MUSQ, the AI coach (up to 10 questions a day), and 2 goal photos a year. It comes as a yearly subscription.

Pro has everything in Premium, with up to 30 questions a day, meal scans that log a meal's calories and macros from a photo (up to 20 a day) and 5 goal photos a year. It comes as a monthly or a yearly subscription, and Pro Yearly starts with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers.

Prices are shown in the app before you buy. The one-time lifetime unlock is no longer sold; if you bought it, you keep Premium.

Do I need an account?

Only for the AI features: Ask MUSQ, meal scans and the goal photo. Sign in with Apple in Profile › Account, and you can hide your email from MUSQ. Everything else in MUSQ works without an account.

What do the AI features send?

Each feature asks for your OK before its first use and names OpenAI. What it needs goes through MUSQ's server to OpenAI, which writes the answer, reads the meal or edits the photo; MUSQ's server doesn't keep your photos, questions or answers. OpenAI may keep what it receives for up to 30 days to check for abuse, and doesn't use it to train its models. Your chat and your goal photo stay on your iPhone. To stop sharing, use Profile › Account › AI data sharing. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my MUSQ account?

Open Profile › Account › Delete account. Apple asks you to confirm once more; MUSQ then asks Apple to end its Sign in with Apple access and deletes your MUSQ account and your plan's record. A few records stay, such as your purchase's usage counts until they expire and the count of your free goal photo; the Privacy Policy lists them. Deleting the app doesn't delete the account. Your workouts stay on your iPhone, and a subscription keeps renewing until you cancel it (see below).

I bought Premium or Pro (or Remove Ads) but it isn't active

Open Profile › MUSQ Premium and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. It can take a moment for a new purchase to reach the app. An earlier Remove Ads purchase counts as Premium. If an AI feature still says it needs a plan right after you buy, wait a moment and try again: MUSQ's server checks your purchase with Apple.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, choose MUSQ and tap Cancel Subscription. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. You can also open this screen from Profile › MUSQ Premium › Manage Subscription in the app (the row says MUSQ Pro if you have Pro).

What happens when my plan ends?

What the plan unlocked locks again, and ads return unless you have Lifetime Premium. Nothing is deleted: your workouts, history, saved presets, chat, logged meals and goal photo stay on your iPhone.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my workout history stored?

On your iPhone. MUSQ doesn't upload your workouts or keep them on a server, and deleting the app deletes your history. The one exception is the AI coach: when you ask it something, your last two weeks of workouts go with the question, through MUSQ's server to OpenAI, once you've agreed.

Something the AI made looks wrong or harmful

On a goal photo, tap Report this image, or delete it. On a coach answer, press and hold it and tap Report this answer. For anything else, email us. AI can be wrong: check a meal estimate before you log it, and for pain or a health condition, see a doctor.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where the law requires it, use Profile › Privacy choices. To stop apps from using your device's advertising identifier, turn off Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking › Allow Apps to Request to Track.
